* 6 Steps to Draw Anything - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T_-DiAzYBc

** Structuralization
   - draw basic primitives in perspective
** Manipulation
   - stretch
   - squash
   - bend
   - curve
   - manipulate contours
** Observation
   - find basic manipulated shapes from life
** Education
   - anatomy
   - machinary
   - architecture
** Imitation
   - find previous principles in artists you like
   - master studies
   - include pieces in your own designs
** Imagination

* Studying Subjects
   - Study a single subject intensely for a week or two at a time
   - This could be something of interest, or your current biggest weakness
** Process
   - Start from life
   - break down into simplest forms
   - turn those around in space
   - light them
   - do them from memory
   - break down into more complex forms
   - turn in space
   - light them
   - do them from memory
   - break down into final forms
   - turn in space
   - light them
   - details and rendering
   - do them from memory
   - learn about them (anatomy, education, function, etc.)
   - repeat process with favorite artists instead of from life
   - create original final redesigns using illustration process
     - thumbnail sketches
     - value studies
     - final

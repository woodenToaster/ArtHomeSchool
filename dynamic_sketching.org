#+TITLE: Dynamic Sketching - Peter Han
#+AUTHOR: Chris Hogan
#+STARTUP: nologdone

* General Formula
   1. simple forms
   2. construction frame (contours)
   3. apply texture
   4. apply shadows
   5. details
   6. highlights and color

* General Advice
  - if you mess up, note what was wrong and redraw it immediately
